# Empathy Mapping & Learner Persona Reference

## The 4-Quadrant Empathy Map

| Quadrant | Questions to ask |
|---|---|
| **THINK & FEEL** | What matters most to them? What are their fears about learning this topic? What excites them about the outcome? |
| **HEAR** | What do their peers/managers say about this skill gap? What advice have they received? |
| **SEE** | What does their current work environment look like? What tools do they use daily? |
| **SAY & DO** | What do they say about their challenges? What have they already tried to learn this? |

Plus:
- **PAINS:** Frustrations, obstacles, fears
- **GAINS:** Desires, goals, measures of success

---

## Adaptive Questioning Guide

Use these questions based on how much the course creator knows about their learners.

### If they know their audience well:
- "What's the single biggest misconception your learners have about this topic?"
- "What habit or mindset do they need to *unlearn* first?"
- "What does success look like 3 months after completing the course?"

### If they're unsure about their audience:
- "Who is the ONE person you're designing this course for? Describe them."
- "Have you taught this topic before? What questions always come up?"
- "Are your learners taking this voluntarily or as a requirement?"

### Follow-up probes (use when answers are vague):
- "Can you give me an example of that?"
- "Is this true for most of your learners, or just some?"
- "On a scale of beginner to expert, where does your typical learner sit — 1 being total newcomer?"

## Learner Persona Template

After gathering enough information, synthesize into this format:

```
👤 LEARNER PERSONA: [Give them a name, e.g., "Priya"]

Background: [2–3 sentences on who they are]
Current situation: [What they're doing now, what's missing]
Goals: [What they want to achieve]
Frustrations: [What's blocking them]
Learning style: [Preferred formats, time availability]
Success metric: [How they'll know the course worked]

QUOTE (synthesized): "[A sentence that captures their mindset]"
```

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Course creator describes "everyone" as their audience → push for specificity
- Learner persona sounds like the course creator themselves → probe for outside perspective
- Goals are vague ("learn more about X") → push for behavioral outcomes ("be able to do Y")
